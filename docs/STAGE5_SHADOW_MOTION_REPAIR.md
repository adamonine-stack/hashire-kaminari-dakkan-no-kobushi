# Stage 5 Shadow Boxer motion repair

The published campaign maps Stage 5 to enemy_02_shadow_boxer. Stage 4 is Cross Murasame; the unused stage_05_cross resource is not a campaign ordering source.

Shadow keeps fighter/save IDs, 190cm reference height, 149px idle silhouette, cap, black hoodie, black boxing gloves, ripped dark jeans and black sneakers. All legacy poses are separated from neighboring pixels and packed into common 320x256 cells at baseline 240. Standing poses keep the idle height; folded, airborne, ground and raised-arm poses retain body mass instead of stretching to a standing bounding box. Normal attacks now display their contact pose on the combat clock. Blurred/ambiguous supplementary actions are replaced by complete single-person art.

shadow_slip_counter now displays a slip, fully extended counterpunch and return to guard. Akky, Gou and Seiya each have their own matching hit, airborne and down art. Hitstop, timing, damage, gauge cost, cooldown and ordinary guard chip remain unchanged. The counter hitbox/effect height is aligned with the extended fist rather than the knees, and its aura keeps the face/gloves readable. Special KO keeps the receiver flight and landing; keep_special_flight_in_view prevents cropped limbs at the boundary. Normal throws use the existing readable grapple receiver pipeline with complete single-person source art and 76px body separation. Seiya received headfirst landing uses the correct inverse transform for the non-unit sprite scale.

Cross defects discovered during the audit are also repaired: fixed 213px standing reference, complete kick art, dedicated Muei grip/pivot/release and three dedicated held/air/down receiver sets. Six ordinary Cross throws retain their combat contracts. Ordinary throw air/down are selected separately, held fighters face each other and retain 76px separation including at the wall. Dedicated state clears after recovery and escape.

Reproduction tools: build_shadow_motion_v2.py and build_cross_motion_v2.py. Original references, generated art, exact prompts and SHA-256 manifests are in art_sources/shadow_motion_v2 and art_sources/cross_motion_v2. Native and private fresh-browser QA run the same checks. The browser fixtures use the published PCK/WASM without changing their bytes; a temporary private HTML bootstrap selects the test scene. Automated browser and native captures are not manual phone gameplay. Release results and any platform diagnostics are recorded separately in evidence/STAGE5_RELEASE_RESULT.md.
